# ZecPath-AI – Day 54 Optimization Report

## 1. Project Information

**Project:** ZecPath – AI-Powered Job Portal and Automated Hiring Assistant
**Day:** 54
**Deliverable:** 2 – Optimization Report
**Module:** AI Accuracy and Scoring Optimization

## 2. Objective

The objective of this deliverable is to document the evaluation of the existing candidate scoring workflow, identify sources of potentially incorrect hiring recommendations, and define optimization measures to improve scoring reliability.

The report uses the output generated by `improved_ai_accuracy.py` and its synthetic evaluation dataset.

## 3. Problems Identified

The evaluation highlighted the following issues that require attention:

1. **Incomplete assessments:** Candidates may be missing mandatory interview or assessment results.
2. **Demo data:** Demonstration scores should not be treated as verified candidate assessment results.
3. **Inconsistent scores:** A supplied overall hiring score may differ from the score calculated using individual assessment rounds.
4. **Assessment score variation:** Large differences between round scores may indicate that a candidate needs further review.
5. **Risk flags:** Candidates with unresolved risk flags require human review.
6. **Threshold-related decisions:** Candidates near selection or rejection thresholds may require careful evaluation.
7. **Limited validation data:** Synthetic test cases cannot establish the accuracy of the system in real hiring situations.

## 4. Optimization Measures

The Day 54 evaluation script implements the following safeguards:

- Validates assessment scores and restricts valid numeric scores to the range 0–100.
- Recalculates a weighted score using available verified assessment results for diagnostic purposes.
- Checks whether supplied and calculated overall scores differ by more than the configured tolerance of 10 points.
- Identifies missing, incomplete, unverified, or demonstration assessment rounds.
- Flags large assessment score variations exceeding the configured spread of 20 points.
- Applies configurable selection and review thresholds.
- Routes candidates with rejected eligibility status or unresolved risk conditions according to the configured decision rules.
- Records decision explanations, evaluation results, errors, and performance information in a JSON report.

These safeguards are implemented in the evaluation script. They do not automatically modify the existing Day 52 production decision engine.

### Interpretation

The script successfully evaluated all 11 test cases without execution errors.

Six candidates were assigned a review decision because the evaluation rules identified conditions requiring further investigation. Among the five binary decisions used for the reported classification metrics, three matched the expected labels and two did not.

The results show that the evaluation pipeline works, but the classification results leave room for improvement. They do not prove that the system's real-world accuracy has improved.

## 6. Findings and Recommendations

The evaluation identified several areas for further refinement:

- Review false-positive and false-negative cases against their expected outcomes.
- Reassess scoring thresholds using appropriately reviewed historical data.
- Verify that every mandatory assessment has a genuine, completed result before final selection.
- Investigate significant differences between individual round scores and the overall score.
- Retain human review for uncertain cases and unresolved risk flags.
- Test the refined scoring rules against additional representative candidate profiles.
- Compare results against a documented baseline before claiming an accuracy improvement.

## 7. Limitations

The evaluation dataset contains synthetic, illustrative candidate records. The expected labels have not been independently validated against actual hiring outcomes.

The reported accuracy, precision, and recall therefore describe performance on this test dataset only. They should not be interpreted as evidence of equivalent performance on real candidates.

The optimization evaluator is a separate validation component and does not itself change the Day 52 hiring decision engine.

Human oversight remains necessary for real hiring workflows.

## 8. Conclusion

Day 54 Deliverable 2 documents the existing scoring safeguards, evaluation results, identified decision errors, and areas requiring further optimization.

The evaluator processed all 11 test cases successfully with zero execution errors. However, the results indicate that additional refinement and validation are necessary before making claims about improved hiring accuracy.

The next step is to refine the scoring logic, validate the changes using representative reviewed data, and compare the results with a documented baseline.
