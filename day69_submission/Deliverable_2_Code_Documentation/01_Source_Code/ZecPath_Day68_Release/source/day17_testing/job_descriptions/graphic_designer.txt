JOB TITLE: Graphic Designer

JOB SUMMARY:
We are looking for a creative Graphic Designer with 3–5 years of experience in creating visual content, branding materials, digital designs, and marketing graphics. The candidate should have strong design skills and the ability to understand client requirements and deliver creative visual solutions.

REQUIRED EXPERIENCE:
Minimum 3 years of experience in graphic design.

REQUIRED SKILLS:
- Adobe Photoshop
- Adobe Illustrator
- Figma
- Canva
- Graphic Design
- UI Design
- Branding
- Video Editing
- Visual Communication
- Layout Design
- Typography

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Create logos, posters, brochures, banners, and social media graphics.
- Develop brand identity and marketing materials.
- Create user interface designs and visual assets.
- Use Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Illustrator for graphic design.
- Use Figma for UI and digital design projects.
- Understand client requirements and prepare creative design concepts.
- Maintain visual consistency across projects.
- Collaborate with marketing and development teams.

EDUCATION:
Bachelor's degree in Fine Arts, Graphic Design, Visual Communication, or a related field is preferred.

JOB TYPE:
Full-time

EXPERIENCE LEVEL:
Intermediate
